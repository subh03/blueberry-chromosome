## Pitch notes (live finals, 3 minutes)

- Fits Manyavar's stated goals: same-store sales were down 5.8% in Q2 FY27, and management wants +1 to 1.5% conversion a year and better-trained staff.
- ₹7,60,100 per 28 days across 20 stores is about ₹99 lakh a year if it holds (simple multiplication; say it is an estimate).
- Next steps, if asked: a store manager view with a "done" button, Hindi cards (13 of the 20 stores are in Lucknow, Kanpur, Patna and Muzaffarpur), and login so each manager sees only their own stores.

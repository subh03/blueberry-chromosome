## Judging (out of 100, same 4 criteria in both rounds)
- 30 · It works: it runs; the main flow finishes without breaking.
- 25 · Use case: a real person has this problem and would use this.
- 20 · Depth: there is real work behind the first screen.
- 25 · Demo and pitch: round 1 = how well the video and submission show the product. No video = max 10 of 25.
**Round 1 (16:45-18:00):** two mentors grade link + repo + video. Nobody pitches. Top 10 announced 17:50.
**Finals (18:00-19:30):** top 10 present LIVE to judges, scored on paper on the same 4 criteria; highest total wins. Winners 19:30.
Judges: Dr Vikash Goel (Founder, Omnifin, fintech), Hemant Chabria (CEO, Chabria Infotech, IT services), Skannd Tyagi (emerging tech, AIC Techno). Expect business-value and "is it real tech" questions.
Prizes: Rs 50,000 / 30,000 / 20,000 (bank transfer within 7 days, TDS cut; PAN + bank details needed).
Company pitch (if on a company statement) runs 16:45-18:00 in parallel with round 1; separate, no score change.
